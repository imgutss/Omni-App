Offline Iranian DNS candidate packs

iran_full.cidr and iran_lite.cidr are unmodified CIDR data snapshots from
anonvector/SlipNet commit 37c669f815fa8c9a7a83968bba5d21c9b07574f5,
app/src/main/assets/geo/ir_dns.cidr and ir_dns_lite.cidr.
SlipNet is licensed under GNU AGPL v3; its license is included beside this
notice. Omni does not copy SlipNet's UI or scanner implementation.

These are address-space candidates, not a claim that each address runs DNS,
not measured resolvers, and not a guarantee of complete Iranian coverage.
Every generated address remains untested until Omni's real DNS probe and,
when requested, native end-to-end tunnel test measures it.
